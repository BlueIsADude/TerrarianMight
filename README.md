Terrarian Might is a mod that adds content from the hit indie game Terraria, without overshadowing or overlapping with any of Minecraft's content.

For Terraria-themed GUI, check out my resource pack, [Terrarian Spirit](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/terrarian-spirit)!

## Budding Life Crystals and Life Shards
**Budding Life Crystals** can be found around the diamond level of the underground, and will slowly grow **Life Crystal Clusters**. These can be mined with a regular pickaxe to get **Life Shards**, or a Silk Touch pickaxe to be used as decoration. Budding Life Crystals cannot be mined with silk touch, and there is no way to accelerate crystal growth. Unlike Budding Amethyst however, Budding Life Crystals will also drop Life Shards when broken, albeit not as many.

## Life Crystals
Life Shards can be crafted together in a heart-shaped formation to create a **Life Crystal**, which can increase your max life by **1 heart** _[health gained is configurable]_, and caps out at a grand total of **20 hearts** _[health cap is also configurable]_. Here's the catch however... the health boost is **not permanent**. As soon as you die, your max health is **reset back to its default values**. This encourages players to try and stay alive, or to explore caves for more Life Crystals.
> If you're running low on storage space, you can pack 9 Life Crystals into a Block of Life Crystal!

## Life Fruit
Life Fruit is a new food item, crafted with Life Shards and Glow Berries. While it doesn't offer much in saturation, it makes up for that by applying Health Boost 5 for 30 seconds _[amplifier and duration are configurable]_ when eaten. However, it has a cooldown of about 5/6ths of the effects duration, and can only be eaten if you have max Life Crystal health.

## Heart Lantern
Surrounding a Lantern with 4 Life Shards will net you a **Heart Lantern**! This moody little light source will provide a bit of regen when interacted with, but will then proceed to crack, reducing the amount of light it gives off. The Heart Lantern's regen abilities can be used a total of 3 times, before eventually becoming a Broken Heart Lantern. Broken Heart Lanterns do not give off light, and do not offer any sort of regen. You can repair your damaged Heart Lanterns using Life Shards.
